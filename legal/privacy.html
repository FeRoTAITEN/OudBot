Privacy Policy

Discord Voice Bridge · Last updated: June 13, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how the Discord Voice Bridge bot ("the Bot", "we", "the Service") handles information when you use it in a Discord server. The Bot is typically operated by a self-hosted administrator ("Operator") on their own infrastructure.

1. Information We Process

Depending on how the Bot is used, the following data may be processed:

DataPurposeStored?
Discord user ID, username, display name Commands, panel access, music requests, per-user mute May be stored locally by the Operator
Discord server (guild) ID, channel IDs, role IDs Voice routing, room assignment, access control Yes — local config files
Voice audio (live stream) Voice bridging between channels No — processed in real time only
Music queries, queue, playlists, playback history Music player features Yes — local files per room
Bot profile settings (avatar, status, etc.) Bot appearance and presence Yes — local config
Language preference Arabic / English UI Yes — per user, locally

2. What We Do Not Collect

  • We do not require or store email addresses, passwords, or payment information
  • We do not sell personal data
  • We do not intentionally record or archive voice conversations
  • We do not operate a central cloud database for all users — data stays on the Operator's server

3. Local Data Storage

When self-hosted, the Operator's machine may store configuration and usage data in local files, including:

  • data/bots.json — bot tokens (sensitive; Operator responsibility)
  • data/bot-channels.json — voice/text channel assignments
  • data/profiles.json — bot profile settings
  • data/playlists/ — saved playlists
  • data/history/ — music playback history

The Operator is responsible for securing these files and complying with applicable privacy laws in their jurisdiction.

4. Third-Party Services

The Bot may communicate with third parties to provide features:

  • Discord — platform API and voice infrastructure (Discord Privacy Policy)
  • YouTube — music search and playback
  • Lyrics providers — Genius, Musixmatch, LRCLib, and others when lyrics are requested

These services receive only the data necessary to fulfill requests (e.g., search queries, video IDs). Their own privacy policies apply.

5. Discord API Data

Use of the Bot is also subject to Discord's Privacy Policy and Developer Policy. We access Discord data only as needed to operate the Bot's features.

6. Data Retention

Locally stored data is retained until deleted by the Operator or removed via Bot features (e.g., clearing playlists or bot settings). Voice data is not retained after a session ends.

7. Your Rights

Depending on your location, you may have rights to access, correct, or delete personal data. Because data is stored locally by each Operator, contact your server's administrator or the Bot Operator to exercise these rights.

8. Children's Privacy

The Bot is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. Discord requires users to meet its minimum age requirements.

9. Security

We take reasonable measures to protect data processed by the Bot. Operators should keep bot tokens and server credentials confidential and restrict panel access using Discord permissions and CONTROL_USER_IDS settings.

10. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. The "Last updated" date above will reflect changes. Continued use of the Bot after changes constitutes acceptance of the revised policy.

11. Contact

For privacy-related questions, contact the Bot Operator or use the contact link provided in the Bot's control panel or music interface.
